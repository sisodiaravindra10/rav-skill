# Ravindra's defaults

An example `dna.md`: the personal defaults of the designer who made this skill. It shows the shape of the file. Do not apply it to anyone else's work.

**Apply only to his own surfaces:** his portfolio, his own products and apps, things built for him personally. Client work, mock projects and generic asks never get it.

**Each of his projects already has its own world, and that world wins.** His live portfolio is one sun, morning blue to chalk to night, a wide display face, mono for data only. Another of his sites is obsidian and acid green on purpose. Read a project's DESIGN.md or tokens before touching it and build inside what is there.

**The defaults apply only to a new personal surface that has no world yet:**
- Surface: paper `#FFFFFF` light or `#0F0F12` dark. Ink `#0A0A0A` is text, not a surface.
- Type: Space Grotesk display, Inter body, JetBrains Mono labels.
- Accent: orange `#F58549`, scarce. A 10x10 signature square, a 2px active border on tabs, one CTA per view.

**What holds across everything of his, whatever the world:**
- Voice: terse, specific, no em-dashes, no fluff words, never the section-sign glyph (U+00A7); write "section 4".
- Brand moments lean cinematic and physical (a corrugated shutter, weight, anticipation, 2 to 4 seconds). Offered a snappy option and a cinematic one for a personal moment, lead with the cinematic one. Tool chrome stays snappy.
- Product and software marketing is bold and product-led: color field, huge type, the real product large.
- Photographs keep their subject clear. On his portfolio no text sits over a photo.
- Indian context is specific when it is there: Jodhpur, not "a desert city".
- British or Indian English, consistently.
