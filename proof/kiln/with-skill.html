The weekend class

Wheel-throwing for first-timers, on Saturdays and Sundays. You spend it at a wheel with your hands in clay, doing these four things in this order.

  1. Wedge

    Knead the clay until it is even all the way through. A lump you miss now turns up later as a lopsided wall.

  2. Centre

    Lean in and hold steady until the spinning lump stops pushing back. This is the hard part, and nobody gets it first go.

  3. Open

    Press a thumb into the middle and stop about a finger above the wheel. That finger of clay is the floor of your bowl.

  4. Pull

    Squeeze the wall between your fingertips and bring it up slowly. Three pulls in, it starts to look like a cup.
